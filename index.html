Finite Differences, Probability, and Information Flows

How do probability distributions change? Can we differentiate entropy on a finite lattice? How does information flow through a network?

Study these questions through finite differences, summation, probability lattices, information gradients, and flows through graphs. The mathematics begins with algebra; logarithms, probability, and angles are developed along the way.

Preparation

Fractions, powers, simple equations, and coordinate graphs. The course introduces its probability, matrix notation, logarithms, and trigonometry. Calculus is not a prerequisite.

A weekly rhythm

Work through the reading and examples, explore the demonstration, complete the five-part lab, and use the eight-question quiz to check understanding. Allow roughly four to six hours of independent work each week.
