Investigation 10.1

For the three-node walk in the reading, verify the row and column sums and compute three updates from \((1,0,0)\). Check that uniform is stationary. Compare this with a cyclic permutation, which preserves uniform but has periodic trajectories.

Investigation 10.2

In the demonstration, begin with its two four-label tables and apply within-pair noise at levels \(0\), \(0.25\), and \(0.5\). Record both directed relative entropies, overlap, and spherical distance. Explain why the same channel must be applied to both tables.

Investigation 10.3

Compare the two offered groupings of the four labels. Find a grouping that erases the distinction between the default models and a grouping that retains it. Explain the result through the ratios inside each group.

Investigation 10.4

Derive the log-sum inequality from Gibbs’ inequality, then use it to prove the relative-entropy data-processing bound. Separately prove overlap increases using Cauchy–Schwarz.

Investigation 10.5

Prepare a capstone report using two positive finite models of your choice. Specify a recording channel, show input and output tables, compute all discrepancies, and interpret the information retained. Include one example showing why entropy alone need not increase under a general channel.
