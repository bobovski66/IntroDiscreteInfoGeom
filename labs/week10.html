Investigation 10.1

On A–B–C with \(p=(1,0,0)\) and \(\tau=1/4\),

  1. compute both signed edge currents \(J_{AB},J_{BC}\),
  2. calculate outward divergence at A, B, C,
  3. use \(\Delta p=-\operatorname{div}J\) to determine the next state, and
  4. check mass conservation.

Investigation 10.2

Using the same path, calculate \(p_1\) through \(p_4\).

  1. Write each state as a probability vector.
  2. Compute all coordinate differences.
  3. Verify every difference vector sums to zero.
  4. Explain the approach toward uniform.

Investigation 10.3

For \(p_0=(1,0,0)\) and the first three transport updates,

  1. compute entropy at each step,
  2. calculate its first differences,
  3. identify the sign of every difference, and
  4. explain which property of the diffusion matrix supports the pattern.

Investigation 10.4

Compare two channels: the symmetric path diffusion and a channel sending every input to A.

  1. Construct each stochastic matrix.
  2. Calculate both output distributions from uniform input.
  3. Compare input/output entropy.
  4. Explain why a general stochastic channel does not have to raise entropy.

Investigation 10.5

Capstone: choose a four- or five-node undirected graph and an initial probability distribution.

  1. Define a legal diffusion step using \(\tau\leq1/d_{\max}\).
  2. Calculate at least four iterates with a table or short program.
  3. Show currents, divergence, mass conservation, and entropy changes.
  4. Compare two states by Fisher–Rao distance and explain what the mathematical results say about the flow of information.
