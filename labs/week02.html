Payoff tables and finite selection

Record calculations, predictions, and observations. Answers are saved in this browser when storage is available; use the download button to keep a separate copy.

Investigation 2.1

For the coexistence table \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2&4\\3&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), derive both average scores, their difference, and the mean score. Find all fixed points of the linear and exponential rules.

Investigation 2.2

At initial proportion \(0.25\), compare five steps of the linear and exponential rules with intensity \(1\). Record values in a table. Explain why agreement on the direction of the first step does not imply agreement on the trajectory.

Investigation 2.3

For exponential coexistence, compare intensities \(0.2\), \(1\), and \(2\). Record the first step that crosses \(0.75\), if one occurs in the first twenty steps. State your findings as observations over a specified run.

Investigation 2.4

Use the coordination table \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4&1\\2&3\end{smallmatrix}\right)\) and initial proportions \(0.45\) and \(0.55\). Predict the long-run direction using the score difference, then check twenty steps.

Investigation 2.5

Add a common score of five to the coexistence table. Derive why the exponential update is unchanged and why the linear update at intensity \(1\) becomes the original linear update at intensity \(1/6\). Verify at one state.
