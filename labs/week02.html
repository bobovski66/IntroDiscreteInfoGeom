Difference equations, feedback, and stability

Investigation 2.1

Consider \(\Delta x_n=\tfrac12(10-x_n)\) with \(x_0=2\).

  1. Rewrite as \(x_{n+1}=F(x_n)\).

  2. Find the fixed point.

  3. Calculate \(x_1,x_2,x_3\).

  4. Find the errors relative to the fixed point and their ratio.

Investigation 2.2

Study \(\Delta x_n=-k(x_n-1/2)\) from \(x_0=1/4\).

  1. Compute three steps for \(k=1/2\).

  2. Repeat for \(k=3/2\).

  3. Describe which sequence crosses the target and why.

  4. Check the prediction from \(|1-k|\).

Investigation 2.3

Use the same equation with \(k=2\) and \(k=5/2\).

  1. Compute four steps.

  2. Explain how the signs and sizes of the errors change.

  3. State why a large corrective step can destabilize a discrete system.

Investigation 2.4

With payoff matrix \(M=((2,4),(3,1))\), write \(f_A(x)\) and \(f_B(x)\) and solve \(f_A=f_B\).

  1. Evaluate the sign of \(f_A-f_B\) at \(x=1/4\) and \(x=9/10\).

  2. Use the original demo to compare finite updates with two intensities.

  3. Explain why the sign test does not give the step size.

Investigation 2.5

Choose an update \(x_{n+1}=F(x_n)\) on \([0,1]\).

  1. List four iterates for two nearby initial states.

  2. Compute each first-difference sequence.

  3. Give numerical evidence for attraction or repulsion and distinguish this from a proof.
