Relative entropy and discrete Lyapunov theory

Investigation 6.1

Let \(p=(3/4,1/4)\) and \(q=(1/2,1/2)\).

  1. Compute \(D(p\|q)\) exactly as a logarithmic expression.

  2. Compute \(D(q\|p)\).

  3. Explain why the two differ.

Investigation 6.2

Use the mixture update \(p_{n+1}=\tfrac12p_n+\tfrac12q\), with \(p_0=(3/4,1/4)\).

  1. Calculate \(p_1,p_2,p_3\).

  2. Check each is a probability vector.

  3. Calculate the errors \(p_n-q\).

Investigation 6.3

Using the sequence from Investigation 2,

  1. calculate \(V_n=D(p_n\|q)\) for \(n=0,1,2,3\),

  2. compute \(\Delta V_0,\Delta V_1,\Delta V_2\), and

  3. test \(V_{n+1}\leq\tfrac12 V_n\) numerically.

Investigation 6.4

Prove by induction that \(p_n-q=(1-\lambda)^n(p_0-q)\) for a general mixture parameter \(\lambda\).

  1. Verify at \(n=0\).

  2. Show the step from \(n\) to \(n+1\).

  3. Explain why the formula establishes convergence for \(0<\lambda\leq1\).

Investigation 6.5

Treat \(V_n\) as a sequence of potential values.

  1. Write its first three dissipation terms \(V_n-V_{n+1}\).

  2. Sum them.

  3. Explain why the cancellation identity measures total information discrepancy lost over three updates.
