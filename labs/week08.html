Week 8 · Lab

Geodesic steps and shortest graph paths

Record calculations, predictions, and observations. Answers are saved in this browser when storage is available; use the download button to keep a separate copy.

Investigation 8.1

For \(p=(1,0)\) and \(r=(1/4,3/4)\), find the ordinary and spherical midpoints. Verify that the latter has equal spherical distances to the two endpoints.

Investigation 8.2

Construct five equally spaced states along a spherical path between two three-label tables. Calculate all four consecutive distances and show that their sum equals the endpoint distance, up to rounding.

Investigation 8.3

In the demonstration, choose denominator \(6\) and two different lattice vertices. Record the endpoints, the direct distance, the shortest graph length, and every count triple along the route. Verify that each transition moves one token.

Investigation 8.4

Reproduce Dijkstra’s algorithm on the five-edge graph in the reading. Record the order of finalized vertices and the predecessor table. Explain the role of nonnegative edge weights in finalizing a distance.

Investigation 8.5

Choose endpoints that lie on both denominator-\(4\) and denominator-\(8\) lattices. Compare their shortest graph lengths with direct distance. Describe the measured result without assuming that every change in denominator creates a nested lattice.
