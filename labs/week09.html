Investigation 9.1

Let \(p=(1/2,1/3,1/6)\), scores \(s=(0,1,2)\), and intensity \(\eta=\ln2\).

  1. Find the three unnormalized weights.
  2. Normalize.
  3. Compute each coordinate of \(\Delta p\).
  4. Verify that the three differences sum to zero.

Investigation 9.2

Take \(p=(1/2,1/2)\), score difference \(d=\ln2\), and \(\eta=1\).

  1. Calculate the next A probability.
  2. Calculate \(\Delta x\).
  3. Find the initial and new odds.
  4. Verify \(\Delta\log O=\eta d\).

Investigation 9.3

Using your three-category example, compute \(J(q)=\eta q\cdot s-D(q\|p)\) for the original p and for the exponential optimizer r.

  1. Evaluate both.
  2. Find \(Z\).
  3. Check \(J(r)=\ln Z\).

Investigation 9.4

Repeat the three-category update for \(\eta=0.1\) and \(\eta=0.5\).

  1. Compute the actual coordinate changes.
  2. Find \(\eta p_i(s_i-\bar s)\) at each intensity.
  3. Compare approximation errors and explain why the smaller step agrees better.

Investigation 9.5

Design scores for which one category begins rare but becomes more likely.

  1. Choose a valid positive starting table.
  2. Calculate four updates.
  3. List the probability differences and the score-weighted means.
  4. Explain what each number reveals about the dynamics.
