Week 9 · Lab

Exponential updates and KL-penalized learning

ReadingDemonstrationLabQuiz

Record calculations, predictions, and observations. Answers are saved in this browser when storage is available; use the download button to keep a separate copy.

Investigation 9.1

Use current table \((1/2,1/3,1/6)\) and scores \((0,1,2)\). Calculate the exponential update at intensity \(\ln2\). Verify its normalizing constant and its log-odds increments.

Investigation 9.2

In the demonstration, compare intensities \(0.1\), \(0.5\), \(1\), and \(2\). For each, record the new table, average score, \(D(r\|p)\), and objective. Explain the balance between reward and departure from the initial table.

Investigation 9.3

Choose three candidate tables, including the current table and a pure table. Check \(J(q)=\ln Z-D(q\|r)\) for each. Use nonnegativity to explain why the computed optimizer beats every candidate, not just those tested.

Investigation 9.4

For fixed scores \((0,1,1)\), compare four rounds from equal initial weights and from \((1/2,1/3,1/6)\). Explain the retained ratio of the tied leaders and prove the direction of the average-score change using the double-sum identity.

Investigation 9.5

Choose two rounds of external scores and reverse their order. Compare intermediate and final tables. Then explain why this rearrangement argument need not hold when scores are computed from the current state.
