Investigation 4.1

Use \(K=((3/4,1/4,0),(1/4,1/2,1/4),(0,1/4,3/4))\).

  1. Check every column sum.
  2. Starting with \(p_0=(1,0,0)\), calculate \(p_1\) and \(p_2\).
  3. Write the two vector differences \(\Delta p_0,\Delta p_1\).

Investigation 4.2

On the path A–B–C, suppose one quarter of the mass at each vertex travels to each adjacent vertex.

  1. Find net current on each edge for \(p_0=(1,0,0)\).
  2. Compute divergence at each vertex.
  3. Verify that minus divergence agrees with \(\Delta p_0\).

Investigation 4.3

Compare \(p=(1/2,1/3,1/6)\) with a direct count sample of 12 outcomes.

  1. Give one integer count triple exactly matching those probabilities.
  2. Give another feasible sample count triple.
  3. Explain why two samples need not equal their expected counts.

Investigation 4.4

Find a fixed point for the three-node matrix.

  1. Verify the proposed vector sums to one.
  2. Multiply by K to check stationarity.
  3. Compare with the cyclic permutation of three labels, which can be stationary at uniform while other orbits rotate.

Investigation 4.5

Design a two-vertex network with a transition probability \(a\) from A to B and \(b\) from B to A.

  1. Construct the column-stochastic matrix.
  2. Write \(\Delta p_A\) in terms of \(p_A,p_B\).
  3. Find a stationary distribution when \(a+b>0\).
